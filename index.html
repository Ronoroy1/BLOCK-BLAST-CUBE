01Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or submit personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or mailing address to play Block Blast Cube.

We do not operate our own user-tracking servers, and we do not directly collect, sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

02Local On-Device Storage

All gameplay progress is stored strictly on your device using Android’s local storage so that you can enjoy Block Blast Cube smoothly even offline. Locally saved data includes:

  • Normal Mode and Pro Mode scores, high scores, and unlocked levels
  • Unlocked medal milestones and in-game skill percentage
  • Virtual in-game cash balance, power-ups, and unlocked block skins/themes
  • Sound effects, music, and haptic vibration preferences

03Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

Block Blast Cube integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, earning bonus in-game virtual cash, level reward multipliers, or board revives).

When ads are requested or displayed, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and interaction information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Such as the Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Network & Approximate Location: IP address and coarse location derived from IP
  • App Activity & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, clicks, device model, OS version, and crash or performance metrics used for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention

This data is collected and handled directly by Google in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy and Advertising Policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google Uses Data from Partner Apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Manage Ad Preferences: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads anytime on your Android device via Settings → Google → Ads.

04Device Permissions

Block Blast Cube requests only the minimal permissions required for core gameplay and ad delivery:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly to fetch and display advertisements via Google AdMob and verify network availability.
  • VIBRATE: Used strictly to deliver optional tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (can be turned off anytime in the game’s Settings menu).

Block Blast Cube does not request or access your camera, microphone, precise GPS location, contacts, SMS, call logs, calendar, or photo/media gallery.

05Virtual Currency & In-Game Rewards

Any virtual cash, reward multipliers, power-ups (such as reverses or revives), skins, themes, or medals earned within Block Blast Cube exist solely for entertainment and in-game progression. They hold zero real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged, redeemed, or transferred for real currency.

06Children’s Privacy

Block Blast Cube is a family-safe puzzle game intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country or region). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

07Data Security

We take your privacy seriously. By storing gameplay progress locally on your device without requiring personal accounts, we minimize data exposure. Any data exchanged by the Google AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS security protocols.

08Your Choices & Data Deletion

Because we do not store your gameplay profile or personal data on external developer servers, you remain in full control of your local game data at all times:

  • Delete Local Data: Clear app storage via Android Settings → Apps → Block Blast Cube → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app to permanently remove all saved scores, levels, and preferences.
  • Ad Controls: Opt out of personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID in your Android device’s Google Ads settings.

09Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to Block Blast Cube or changes in legal or platform requirements. Any revisions will be posted directly on this page with an updated Effective Date at the top. Continued use of the game after updates signifies your acknowledgment of the revised policy.
